About me

Hello, I'm Anushree

My journey with Mandala art began in the most unexpected way — during lockdown, when my sister (the more artistic one between us!) sat down with me and taught me my very first Mandala. I had no idea what I was doing, but something about it just clicked.

The repetition, the symmetry, the quiet focus it demanded — it made me concentrate in a way nothing else had before. It was calming and fun all at once. I was so hooked that I went out the very next day and bought all my supplies, and I haven't stopped since.

Over time, I opened an art account and started sharing my work online, slowly building a little community around this beautiful practice. And now, with a bit of experience and a whole lot of love for what Mandala art has done for me, I want to pass that on to kids — because if it helped me concentrate and find calm, I truly believe it can do the same for them.

Sessions are kept small and personal, so every child feels seen and supported. No prior experience needed — just curiosity and a willingness to slow down and create something beautiful.
