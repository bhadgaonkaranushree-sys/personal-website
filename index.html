About me

Hello, I'm Anushree

I'm a Mandela artist and community educator based in your city. What began as a personal practice of calm and focus has grown into something I love sharing — especially with young minds.

Mandela art combines geometry, symmetry, and patience. For children, it's not just a drawing exercise — it builds concentration, fine motor skills, and a quiet confidence in their own creative expression.

I run small, intimate sessions from my studio, welcoming children of all skill levels. No experience needed — just curiosity and a willingness to sit with something beautiful.
